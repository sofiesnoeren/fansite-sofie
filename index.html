The Marvel Cinematic Universe is an American media franchise and shared universe centered on a series of superhero films produced by Marvel Studios, based on characters from American comic books published by Marvel Comics.

Examples of Marvel Superheroes are:

- Iron Man

- Captain America

- Spiderman

- Black Widow

- Thor
